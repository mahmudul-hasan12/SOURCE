# SkySourcing 1688 & Taobao Chrome Extension

This Chrome Extension empowers you to browse **1688.com**, **Taobao.com**, and **Tmall.com** directly in China and import products into your Bangladeshi e-commerce store with a single click.

## How to Install in Google Chrome:

1. Open **Google Chrome** on your computer.
2. In the URL bar, go to: `chrome://extensions/`
3. In the top-right corner, toggle on **Developer mode**.
4. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left corner.
5. Select this folder:
   ```
   C:\Users\TAWHID TOPON\Documents\google\extension
   ```
6. The extension is now installed! You will see the **🚀 SkySourcing** icon in your Chrome extensions bar.

## Features:
- **Instant Product Capture**: Automatically grabs high-resolution image galleries, product titles, 1688 tiered wholesale pricing (e.g., 2–9 pcs, 10–49 pcs, 50+ pcs), and SKU variations (colors, sizes, styles).
- **Live BDT Currency Conversion**: Real-time RMB-to-BDT calculation with your configurable exchange rate and profit margin.
- **Direct Database Sync**: Sends the product straight to your Next.js store backend (`http://localhost:3000/api/extension/import`).
- **Floating One-Click Button**: Floating quick-import badge injected directly onto 1688 and Taobao offer pages.
